Overview

POS Cash In/Out Multi Currency & Receipt extends the standard Odoo Point of Sale cash management process by allowing cash deposits and withdrawals in multiple currencies.

Configure allowed currencies per POS, perform cash in/out using the selected foreign currency, automatically convert amounts to company currency, print professional transaction receipts, and track foreign currency details in the cash register and journal entries.

Key Features

  • Configure allowed currencies per POS shop.
  • Perform Cash In operations in foreign currencies.
  • Perform Cash Out operations in foreign currencies.
  • Automatic conversion to company currency using Odoo exchange rates.
  • Foreign currency tracking in cash register statements.
  • Foreign currency tracking in journal entries.
  • Dedicated Cash In/Out receipt screen with print option.
  • One cash payment method per POS for cash in/out (validation enforced).
  • Fully integrated with Odoo POS sessions.
  • Odoo 19 compatible.

How It Works

1. Enable Cash In/Out Payment Method

Enable Use For POS Cash In/Out on exactly one cash payment method linked to your POS shop.

2. Configure Allowed Currencies Per POS

Choose which currencies can be used for Cash In/Out transactions in each Point of Sale configuration. The shop currency is selected by default in the cash in/out popup.

3. Cash Out Example – USD

Perform Cash In/Out transactions directly in USD or any configured currency from the POS interface.

4. Cash Out Example – INR

Perform Cash In/Out transactions directly in INR or any other currency configured for that POS shop.

5. Receipt Printing

After confirmation, view the transaction on a dedicated receipt screen and print a professional Cash In/Out receipt with transaction details and the selected currency amount.

6. Cash Register Tracking

Track all Cash In/Out operations in the cash register with foreign currency details and converted company currency amounts.

7. Journal Entries

Automatic accounting entries with foreign currency information and company currency conversion for accurate bookkeeping.

Why Use This Module?

  • Manage multi-currency cash drawers easily.
  • Reduce manual currency conversion errors.
  • Improve cashier accountability.
  • Maintain proper accounting records.
  • Provide printed proof of cash movements.
  • Streamline end-of-day cash control.

Technical Notes

  • Uses standard Odoo currency exchange rates for conversion to company currency.
  • Supports multiple allowed currencies per POS configuration.
  • Accounting entries are stored in company currency while preserving original foreign currency amounts.
  • Only one cash payment method per POS can be enabled for cash in/out operations.
  • Fully integrated with the native Odoo POS Cash In/Out workflow and session management.
  • Requires Odoo 19.
